🔔 Push Notifications

Self-hosted ntfy is bundled. In Uptime Kuma: Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ Setup Notification, type ntfy, server URL http://127.0.0.1:9090, pick an unguessable topic, press Test, then subscribe to the topic in the ntfy phone app. For alerts that pierce Silent mode, point a webhook at the KumaAlert relay instead.
